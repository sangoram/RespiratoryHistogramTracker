What OWL does
OWL turns bedside saturation histograms into a unit-wide picture. Every bed carries its respiratory support, FiO₂, target range and a cumulative oxygenation tally, so a glance across the board shows where time is being spent below, inside and above target.
More on the approach, and the evidence behind it, is coming from the OWL site — this space is reserved for it.
